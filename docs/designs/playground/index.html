About me

I don’t trust a model I couldn’t write myself. So I write them: attention, RoPE, a KV cache, a VQ-VAE codebook, PPO’s clipped objective, until the shapes line up and the paper makes sense.

That habit became From_Scratch, ten modules of ML, DL and RL built from first principles, from activation functions to diffusion and a spiking network wired from a real fruit-fly connectome, and Language_Model_Implementations, where I rebuilt LLaMA 2, Gemma 3 and Qwen 3 block by block and trained a small language model with its own tokenizer.

I also ship. My applied projects put LLMs and embeddings inside systems that have to be right: retrieval over real codebases, multi-agent pipelines checked in code, semantic search behind Go services. Today I’m a software developer intern at Helionyex Solutions, and I’ve trained 150+ educators in Python and generative AI at NimbleQ.
