About me

I learn deep learning by rebuilding it. Attention, RoPE, a KV cache, a VQ-VAE codebook, PPO's clipped objective: I write each one by hand and check the tensor shapes until the paper makes sense.

That habit became From_Scratch, a repository of ten modules that runs from activation functions to a spiking network wired from a real fruit-fly connectome, and Language_Model_Implementations, where I rebuilt LLaMA 2, Gemma 3 and Qwen 3 block by block and trained a small language model of my own.

Then I put models to work. My projects wrap LLMs and embeddings in systems that can be trusted: retrieval over real codebases, multi-agent pipelines whose outputs are checked in code, and services in Go and FastAPI that stay up under load.

I finished a B.Tech in AI & ML at AKTU in 2026 and work as a software developer intern at Helionyex Solutions. I've also mentored 150+ educators in Python and generative AI at NimbleQ.
